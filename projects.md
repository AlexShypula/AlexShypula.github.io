## Projects

[Who ends up owning AI?](ai-equity/) — an interactive model that traces equity in the leading AI firms (Nvidia, Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Anthropic, OpenAI) through the institutions that hold it to the households that ultimately own the claim. Every pass-through share is an explicit, sourced assumption you can edit; a v1 heuristic set and a v2 researched set can be compared. [Methods and audit notes](https://github.com/AlexShypula/AlexShypula.github.io/blob/main/ai-equity/docs/AUDIT.md).
