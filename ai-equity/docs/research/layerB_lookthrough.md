## 5. Offshore round-tripping literature
- CMNS (QJE 2021) restates by issuer nationality; does not estimate who ultimately owns Cayman funds' US equity. https://www.nber.org/papers/w26855
- Beck, Coppola, Lewis, Maggiori, Schmitz & Schreger, "Geography of Capital Allocation in the Euro Area" (end-2023): Luxembourg funds' equity €2,277bn, 44% owned by non-euro-area investors; Irish funds' equity €1,665bn, 66% non-euro-area; 31% of official euro-area US-equity holdings are actually non-euro-area owned. US residents are a minor part. https://globalcapitalallocation.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/GCAP_ECB_OOFCs.pdf
- Cayman indirect evidence: US investors hold $2,145bn of Cayman equity (TIC SHC YE2024) and $2,172bn of offshore hedge fund shares (Z.1); 53.6% of qualifying hedge fund NAV is Cayman-domiciled (Form PF 2025 Q3); Fed FEDS Note 2025-10-15 found Cayman hedge funds' Treasuries undercounted by ~$1.4T. **No published share of Cayman's $2.16T US equity that is US-owned; agent estimate 40–60% round-tripped (L).**
